## Decision checkpoint
- At requirements freeze: log chosen interpretation and questions.
- At first working slice: log architecture, algorithm, validation and fallback.
- At integration: record what data/tests changed initial intent.
- Before submission: remove ungrounded claims; add open risks and evidence links.
